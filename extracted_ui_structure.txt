QuickRes Frontend — Recommended UI Structure
Purpose: Design the QuickRes frontend to demonstrate an Agentic AI support-ticket resolution system, not a conventional chatbot.
1. Resolver Dashboard — Main Screen
Make this the primary screen.
Top metrics: Total Tickets | Autonomous Resolution Rate | Pending Approvals | Tool/RAG Invocations.
Left: Ticket Queue with search, filters, status, customer tier, and risk indicators.
Right: Selected ticket with customer information, investigation status, evidence, proposed action, and approval state.
Purpose: Show that QuickRes is actively investigating tickets and making controlled decisions.
2. Investigation Trace — Most Important Demo Feature
Use “Investigation Trace” or “Agent Execution Trace”, not “AI's inner thoughts”.
Show an auditable sequence: Investigate → Tool Call → Knowledge Retrieval → Reflection → Risk Assessment → Human Approval when required.
Show concise actions, observations, evidence, and decisions.
Do not expose private chain-of-thought or scratchpad reasoning.
3. Human Approval Center
Display high-risk proposed actions clearly.
Example: Ticket #1042 | Customer: Sarah Connor | Proposed Action: REFUND $49.99 | Risk: HIGH.
Show supporting evidence such as subscription status, payment verification, and the relevant Knowledge Base policy.
Actions: Approve & Execute | Reject | Edit Payload & Approve.
Purpose: Make the Human-in-the-Loop requirement visible.
4. Customer Portal
Keep the interface simple and customer-facing.
Show customer message, QuickRes status, high-level investigation progress, clarification requests, supervisor-review status, and final resolution.
Do not expose tool JSON, risk scores, internal investigation details, internal policies, database information, or private reasoning.
Example statuses: AI Investigator Working… | Additional Details Needed | In Supervisor Review | Resolved.
6. What Should Be Prominent
Resolver Dashboard: Ticket queue, ticket status, risk, agent investigation, evidence, proposed action, human approval.
Investigation Trace: Agent action, selected tool, tool result, retrieval, evidence, reflection result, risk decision.
Customer Portal: Customer message, AI status, clarification, resolution.
8. What Should Not Be Exposed
Do not display private chain-of-thought or scratchpad reasoning.
Instead of: “I think I should probably check the subscription because…”
Show: “Agent Decision: Subscription status was required to determine refund eligibility.”
The frontend should present an auditable execution trace rather than private internal reasoning.
9. Recommended Navigation
QUICKRES
Resolver Dashboard | Customer Portal | Knowledge Base
The Resolver Dashboard should contain the Ticket Queue, Investigation Trace, Evidence, Risk Assessment, and Human Approval workflow.
10. If Only One Screen Can Be Perfected
Prioritize the Resolver Dashboard with Ticket Queue + Investigation Trace + Approval Panel.
This single screen can demonstrate: Agentic decision-making → dynamic tool usage → Agentic RAG → reflection → risk assessment → Human-in-the-Loop → resolution.
The existing backend capabilities should be showcased rather than adding unnecessary frontend features.
